HOW TO PLAY

Lowest card opens. Play the same number of cards at a higher rank, or pass. Valid plays are singles, pairs, triples, straights, flushes, full houses, four of a kind, and straight flushes. Five-card hands rank in that order. Suits rank ♦ ♣ ♥ ♠ and 2 is highest. Empty your hand first: gain one point per card opponents hold while each opponent loses their remaining-card count.
